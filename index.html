About the Show

Celebrating the **250th anniversary of the signing of the Declaration of Independence**. This historic production features over 36 combined studios representing multiple states with around 360 total performers.

Official Show Order:

Skydivers → SOF Dancers "250th Celebration Production Routine" → Color Guard → Freedom Honorees → Nitro Circus & SOF Dancers → Brad Paisley Concert → Drone Sky Show → Fireworks Spectacular.
